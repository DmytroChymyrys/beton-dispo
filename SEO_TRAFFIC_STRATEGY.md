# BétonDispo SEO and Traffic Strategy

## Verdict

BétonDispo can earn organic traffic, but the first leads should not depend on
organic search alone.

Use organic SEO as the long-term acquisition engine and run a small, controlled
Google Ads campaign at launch to generate early leads and keyword data while SEO
matures.

## Why Not Organic Only

- Local SEO usually takes weeks to months to produce meaningful traffic.
- New pages need time to be crawled, indexed and trusted.
- Organic ranking depends on more than technical SEO: domain authority, local
  signals, backlinks, citations, user engagement and search demand all matter.
- The current site has a good SEO foundation, but that does not guarantee quick
  first-page rankings.

Organic-only is possible over time, especially for French long-tail searches,
but it is a slower path for first traction.

## Recommended Launch Approach

Run both channels:

- Organic SEO for compounding long-term traffic.
- Google Search Ads for immediate buyer-intent traffic and conversion data.

Suggested initial ad period:

- 30 to 60 days.
- Small daily budget.
- Strict geographic targeting.
- Track quote submissions and calculator-to-quote clicks.

## Paid Search Focus

Target high-intent searches, especially in French.

Example keyword themes:

- livraison beton
- livraison béton
- béton mobile
- pompage béton
- pompe à béton
- soumission béton
- calculateur béton
- calculateur de béton
- béton rive-sud
- béton montréal
- béton grand montréal

Recommended landing pages:

- Calculator searches: `/fr/calculateur-beton`
- Quote/service searches: `/fr/soumission` or `/fr/services`
- English searches: `/en/concrete-calculator`, `/en/quote`, `/en/services`

Keep campaigns tightly targeted to the actual service area:

- Grand Montréal
- Rive-Sud
- Montréal
- Other confirmed delivery/service regions

## Organic SEO Priorities

Immediate:

- Verify Google Search Console.
- Submit `/sitemap.xml`.
- Confirm production `robots.txt` allows indexing.
- Confirm canonical domain is consistent across `.com` and `.ca`.
- Inspect `/fr/calculateur-beton` in Search Console after launch.

Short term:

- Monitor impressions and queries for calculator and service pages.
- Improve copy based on actual Search Console query data.
- Add internal links from relevant pages to the calculator and quote form.
- Make sure Google Business Profile is created and accurate if applicable.

Medium term:

- Add useful local/service landing pages when there is enough demand.
- Prioritize pages such as:
  - `/fr/livraison-beton/rive-sud`
  - `/fr/livraison-beton/montreal`
  - `/fr/beton-mobile/rive-sud`
  - `/fr/pompage-beton`
- Build real local citations and backlinks from partners, directories,
  associations and relevant construction/local business sources.

## Measurement

Primary conversions:

- Quote form submissions.
- Calculator result to quote click.

Secondary signals:

- Calculator page impressions.
- Calculator page clicks.
- Search queries in Google Search Console.
- Quote page visits from organic and paid traffic.
- Cost per quote request from ads.

Do not judge SEO too early. Use the first few weeks mainly to verify indexing,
query discovery and technical health.

## Bottom Line

The SEO foundation is good. Organic can become the main acquisition channel, but
early-stage local SEO is usually too slow to rely on by itself.

Use a modest Google Ads campaign to create early lead flow and learn which
keywords convert, while continuing to build the organic channel for durable
traffic.
